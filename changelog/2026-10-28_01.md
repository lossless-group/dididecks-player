## Why Care?

Every site in the tree that showed a deck had rebuilt its own viewer, and the
copies had drifted: nine scroll-deck wrappers, four slide canvases, and the
newest fix always in whichever site needed it last. The DidiDecks shell had the
best play and print pieces but couldn't be used outside a dididecks
client-site, because it also carries the slide rater, which writes to disk.

## What's New?

- **A standalone package.** `@lossless-group/dididecks-player` holds the
  viewer: static Astro components, scoped CSS, `--ddd-chrome-*` tokens. No
  Tailwind, no filesystem access, no registry.
- **`DeckPlayer` and `PlayerSlide`.** A single-page play deck: one slide at a
  time, arrow keys and swipe, a contents list that skips to any slide (T or J),
  fullscreen (F), `#N` links to a slide, and print at one slide per page. A
  slide can be an Astro component or a rendered image.
- **`PrintDeck`.** The print layout from the shell's print route, usable
  anywhere.
- **`SlideCanvas` centres its slide.** A non-16:9 space now letterboxes evenly
  on both sides instead of leaving the gap at the right and bottom. It also
  gains calmstorm-decks' optional wash layer.
- **`ContentFit`** joins from calmstorm-decks: scales overflowing content down
  to fit.

## Fixes

- `DeckChrome` listened for `ddd:section-changed`, but the scroll deck sends
  `deck:section-changed`, so the first/last-slide state never updated. It now
  listens for both.
- `DeckChrome` no longer hijacks browser shortcuts: Cmd+C copies instead of
  hiding the chrome.

## What stays in the shell

The rater and triage tools, the deck registry and the `/play` and `/toc`
routes remain in `@dididecks/shell`, which now depends on this package and
re-exports these components at their old paths.
